The issue I want to focus on is the pollution of heavy metal in groundwater or aquatic ecosystems. 
I want to examine how different factors such as pH and temperature can affect contamination.
I also want to compile another set of data on nanoparticle and investigate their adsorption capacities to heavy metals as a mitigation strategy.
This issue is important as heavy metals can damage aquatic organisms as well as human health if consumed into the body.
